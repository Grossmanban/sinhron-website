Sinhron is a messenger where privacy isn’t a setting — it’s a property of the whole system. Messages are encrypted on your phone with the MLS protocol, and the server they pass through belongs to you or to people you trust.

## Why another messenger

We wanted three things at once: end-to-end encryption with no exceptions, a server you can bring up with one command, and an app that’s a pleasure to use every day. Usually you get to pick one.

## What already works

- 1:1 chats and groups, end-to-end encrypted;
- reactions, replies, edit and delete, pinned messages, folders and archive;
- 1:1 audio and video calls;
- disappearing messages, Face ID lock, safety numbers;
- English and Russian, light, dark and black themes.

## What isn’t there yet

We’re in early beta. There are no group calls, no multiple devices per account, no app-store builds and no independent security audit yet. The full list is on the [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap).
