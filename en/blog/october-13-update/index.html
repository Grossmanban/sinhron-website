Mentions, profile photos and screen recording

A long day of work on the app, all of it done without a phone in hand. Here is what changed, and what still needs a real iPhone before we can call it finished.

New

  • @mentions in groups. Type @ and pick a member. A mention points at the person's key, not their name, so it stays correct when names change. If someone mentions you, the chat gets an @ badge, and the notification comes through even when the chat is muted.
  • Profile photos. Set a photo for your profile. It is sent inside the encryption, only to chats you're actually talking in, and it is re-encoded twice, once on your phone and once on theirs, so location and camera details never travel with it.
  • Screen recording notes. In chats with disappearing messages, everyone already saw a note when someone took a screenshot. Now recording or mirroring the screen is noted too, once per recording.

Fixed

We reviewed the whole day's changes and fixed what we found, with a test for each fix. Among them:

  • a new contact could accidentally "accept" their own message request;
  • deleted messages could leave traces in the on-device search index;
  • a screened-out call could tell the caller your phone was online.

Tested

A new automated test runs two complete clients against each other: names, pins, edits and deletes within the 24-hour window, screenshot notes and timers all make the round trip through real MLS encryption (the network and sealed delivery have their own tests). It runs on every change.

Not yet

None of this has been tried on a real iPhone yet. That comes next, together with push notifications, which need an Apple developer account. The Roadmap has the full picture.
