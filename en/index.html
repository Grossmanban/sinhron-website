Security

Built so we can’t look. Not just promising not to.

Privacy in Sinhron isn’t a setting. It’s how the whole thing is built — on open standards you can check.

Always encrypted

There is no unencrypted mode — not for convenience, not for debugging, not for admins.

The server isn’t trusted

Sinhron assumes the server could be hostile. It stores ciphertext and the minimum needed to deliver it.

Private calls

Calls are encrypted end to end, even while they pass through a media server.

Zero telemetry

No analytics, no tracking, no phoning home. Not in the app — and not on this website.

Locked on your device

Your chat history is encrypted on the phone and can be locked behind Face ID.

A key only you hold

Your account is a 24-word key. Lose it and nobody — including us — can recover it.

Check it’s really them

Compare a 60-digit safety number — as digits or a QR code — when you meet. If it matches, nobody is in the middle.

Self-host

Your server. In a minute.

Sinhron runs on a server you trust — yours, your team’s or your family’s. One Docker Compose file brings up everything, with automatic HTTPS.

One command

Server, database, calls and TLS — all in Docker Compose.

Up to 10,000 users

Designed for families, teams and communities on a single host.

Your data stays put

Nothing goes to us. There is no “us” in the loop.
